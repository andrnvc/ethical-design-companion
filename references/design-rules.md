# Operational ethical design rules — v0.1.0
These IDs are authored for this skill, not quotations or an official taxonomy from the source book. Apply proportionally and contextually.

## 01 User autonomy
- AUT-01 Require recognizable, voluntary action for consequential choices.
- AUT-02 Do not make declining, correcting or reversing a decision disproportionately hard.
- AUT-03 Reveal material consequences before commitment.
- AUT-04 Avoid defaults that silently assume consequential or legally required consent.

## 02 Transparency and honesty
- TRN-01 Make CTA labels match the resulting action.
- TRN-02 Disclose material price, fee and condition information at the appropriate decision point.
- TRN-03 Do not invent or misleadingly exaggerate scarcity, demand or urgency.
- TRN-04 Explain consequential personalization or automation sufficiently for user understanding.

## 03 Privacy by design
- PRV-01 Minimize collection to data necessary for the specified purpose.
- PRV-02 Favor privacy-protective defaults.
- PRV-03 Explain relevant data purposes and recipients in comprehensible language.
- PRV-04 Where consent is the legal basis, make it valid and withdrawable; distinguish consent from other legal bases.
- PRV-05 Make applicable privacy rights reasonably discoverable and usable.

## 04 Fairness and respect
- FAR-01 Identify and mitigate unjustified exclusion or discriminatory effects.
- FAR-02 Do not target known vulnerabilities for exploitation.
- FAR-03 Do not selectively conceal decision-relevant information from particular groups.
- FAR-04 Write constructive, respectful feedback and errors, without shaming.

## 05 Non-manipulation
- MAN-01 Avoid fake scarcity or timers.
- MAN-02 Avoid confirmshaming in refusal copy.
- MAN-03 Never silently add purchases, signups or paid extras.
- MAN-04 Avoid deceptive visual emphasis or misdirection between choices.
- MAN-05 Avoid unreasonable obstruction to cancellation, withdrawal or refusal.

## 06 Accessibility and inclusion
- ACC-01 Ensure interaction works with relevant input methods including keyboard.
- ACC-02 Do not convey information by color alone.
- ACC-03 Use understandable labels, instructions and error messages.
- ACC-04 Verify applicable WCAG 2.2 success criteria for contrast, focus, targets, text and semantics; do not claim full conformance from screenshots.
- ACC-05 Provide appropriate control or accommodations for time limits subject to relevant exceptions.

## 07 Digital wellbeing
- WEL-01 Give notifications a defensible user benefit.
- WEL-02 Avoid gratuitous attention interruptions.
- WEL-03 Give users usable control of notification preferences.
- WEL-04 Avoid engagement loops designed to foster harmful compulsive use.
