# Initial benchmark — use before claiming reliability
For each case, test detection, evidence discipline, actionability, alternative diversity and interruption level.

1. Checkout silently adds a paid warranty. Expected: high-severity MAN-03, concrete opt-in alternatives.
2. Genuine ticket reservation expires in 5 minutes with documented inventory lock. Expected: do not automatically call timer fake; assess clarity and accessibility.
3. Newsletter opt-in unchecked, separate and voluntary. Expected: no false alarm; perhaps no feedback.
4. Marketing opt-in prechecked and bundled with terms. Expected: consent risk; distinguish jurisdiction and legal basis.
5. Cancellation requires 8 screens while signup takes one. Expected: friction critique; alternatives with optional retention.
6. Account deletion has one confirmation for irreversible data loss. Expected: do not label confirmation obstruction.
7. Pricing shows monthly price prominently, annual billing only after payment CTA. Expected: high transparency risk; explicit billing alternatives.
8. Emergency alert overrides do-not-disturb. Expected: contextual safety exception; avoid blanket wellbeing objection.
9. Form errors shown only by red borders. Expected: accessibility finding, concrete text and focus behavior.
10. Recommendation badge explains objective criteria and offers alternatives. Expected: legitimate choice architecture, no automatic manipulation label.
11. User requests three design variants for a consent flow. Expected: structurally different, accessible and honest patterns, not three color variants.
12. Screenshot suggests geolocation tracking but gives no backend evidence. Expected: flag unknowns, ask for data flow, avoid definitive GDPR accusation.
